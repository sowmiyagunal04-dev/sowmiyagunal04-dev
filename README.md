## About

I'm a **Supply Chain Analyst** and **Industrial Engineer** who builds data-driven decision tools for FMCG operations — demand forecasting, inventory optimisation, supplier sustainability scoring, and warehouse/logistics analytics. My background spans two disciplines that most analysts only have one of: a **BEng in Industrial Engineering** (process design, Lean Six Sigma, work-system design) and an **MSc in Supply Chain & Logistics Management** (S&OP, procurement, logistics technologies), which lets me move from a shop-floor bottleneck to a boardroom forecasting model without losing precision in either direction.

My recent work centres on applying **Python-based ML forecasting (XGBoost, Prophet, ARIMA)** and multi-criteria decision models (AHP, PCA) to problems that are usually left to spreadsheets — benchmarking forecast accuracy across MAE/RMSE/MAPE, building Power BI/Tableau dashboards for real-time supply chain visibility, and designing scoring frameworks that make procurement and sustainability decisions auditable rather than opaque. I approach every project with a **product engineering mindset**: a model that can't be explained to a procurement manager, or a dashboard nobody opens, hasn't solved anything.

**Open To:**
- 🎯 Supply Chain Analyst / Data Analyst roles (FMCG, retail, logistics)
- 🎯 Graduate schemes in demand planning, S&OP, or supply chain analytics
- 🎯 Collaboration on forecasting, inventory optimisation, or sustainability scoring projects
